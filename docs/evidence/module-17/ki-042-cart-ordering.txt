KI-042 — the same ordering fault in the cart, found by looking rather than by failing
====================================================================================

Found by auditing every controller after KI-041, not by a test failure. Nothing
in CI had failed on it.

The cart has the fault in an easier place to reach than the pickup screen did.
_load() revalidates; _edit() writes; empty() writes; and _revalidateQuietly() is
fired UNAWAITED behind every successful edit. All four assigned CartState.view
from whatever came back, whenever it came back.

The second path needs no slow network. A customer tapping "+" twice has the
first tap's quiet revalidation in flight across the second tap's response; when
it lands it carries the cart as it was before the second tap, and the quantity
drops from 3 back to 2 on screen, over a cart the server has at 3.

A NOTE ON THE SECOND TEST, because it passed on its first draft for no reason.
The gate was armed AFTER the first increment — by which time that edit had
already fired its quiet revalidation and the fake had answered it. The test
exercised nothing and said so by passing. Armed before the first tap, it
reproduces. A test that passes without touching its subject is worth less than
no test, and this one was caught only because the failure it claimed to cover
was already understood.
