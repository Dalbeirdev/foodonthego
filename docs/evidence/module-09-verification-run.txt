Read this way:

  * 14 queries per open, and the number does not move. Twenty more
    photographs, ten more cuisines and ten more facilities produce the same
    14: the relations are eager-loaded, not looped over. Fifteen more
    restaurants on the route produce the same 14 as well.
  * Nine of the fourteen are the discovery half — validating the route and
    rebuilding the cached selection against live rows. Five are the profile:
    one restaurant row and four eager loads. All bounded, none per-relation.
  * The route context came from cache on 10 of 10 opens. Opening a
    restaurant calls no routing provider, and it cannot: the only thing in
    the application that can reach one is already cached before the detail
    service is asked.
  * The eligibility and availability re-check on every open is deliberate.
    It is why a restaurant suspended thirty seconds ago cannot ride a warm
    cache onto a customer's screen.

The 20 extra photographs are inserted by the measurement script and deleted
before it returns.
